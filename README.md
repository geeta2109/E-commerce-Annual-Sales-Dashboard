project Name: E-Commerce Annual Sales Dashboard

Tools Used: Excel / Power BI / Tableau / SQL

Problem:
Analyze annual e-commerce sales performance across different sales channels, product categories, and customer demographics to identify business growth opportunities.

Observations:
Women customers contributed a significantly higher percentage of total sales compared to men.
Major sales were generated from top states such as Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.

Challenges:
Multiple sales channels (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, etc.) made it difficult to consolidate and compare performance data.
Some orders were returned, refunded, or cancelled, affecting overall revenue analysis.

Suggestions:
Focus marketing campaigns on female customers since they contribute the majority of purchases.
Strengthen logistics and product quality control to reduce returns and cancellations.

Conclusion:
The e-commerce business shows strong performance across multiple online marketplaces with a large share of sales from women customers.
Targeted marketing and better order management strategies can help increase revenue and improve customer satisfaction.
